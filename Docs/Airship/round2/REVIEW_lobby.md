# Round 2, PR B: lobby review, verdicts and fixes

Three reviewers (net, rules, ui) sent 16 findings on the lobby code (`SPEC_B_LOBBY.md` rev 2). Each one was checked
against the worktree `_AirshipWorkB` (branch `airship-r2-prb`) and the engine source in `/mnt/new_partition/5.8`.

**Counts.** 16 findings: 15 real, 1 not real as described. Of the real ones, 2 pairs are duplicates (rows 3 and 5, rows 2
and 6). 15 fixed, plus 1 hardening change for the one that is not real. Nothing is left open.

**Build.** `_AirshipWork_logs/build_r2b.sh`: `build_r2b_061106.log` (31 actions) and `build_r2b_061456.log` (the last
edit). Both give Result Succeeded with no new warnings. The only warnings are the old `bIsFocusable` ones in
`MainMenu.cpp` / `MainMenu2.cpp`. Nothing ran in the editor or PIE.
